Privacy & data use

Updated November 9, 2026 · Private household finance app

Casa is an invitation-only household finance tool. Its interface is hosted on GitHub Pages. Financial records, source documents, account details and credentials are not published in that interface or repository.

Sign-in and access

Google sign-in verifies your identity. The backend checks an approved household account list before serving financial data or accepting uploads. A separate one-time owner connection uses Google’s limited drive.file permission to create documents in the configured Inbox. It does not request permission to read your entire Drive.

Where information lives

The master financial records and source documents live in the household’s restricted Google Drive and Google Sheet. Private runtime files hold import drafts, processing receipts, cached summaries and AI usage. Owner upload credentials are encrypted in Google Secret Manager. Browser sign-in tokens and chat conversation history stay in page memory rather than persistent browser storage.

Google AI

Pressing Refresh can send new Inbox documents to Google Gemini for extraction and categorization. PDF and image content may include any identifiers visible in the original; redact or crop those before upload. AI summaries receive aggregate comparisons. Chat receives relevant saved financial figures and a limited sample of transaction descriptions. Recognized identifiers in text are scrubbed, but automatic redaction is not guaranteed.

Google Cloud’s data-governance and retention terms apply. Do not assume zero retention. Casa does not enable model tools, web search, or request-response logging for these financial requests. AI cannot transfer money or directly edit records; Casa validates proposed imports and sends uncertain sources to review.

Processing and retention

Casa records processed file IDs and content hashes to avoid repeated analysis. Original uploads remain in Drive. Removing a source file does not automatically delete imported financial records or processing history. The household owner controls records and access, and can arrange removal of stored records, drafts, summaries or the upload connection. Limited operational and security logs may be retained by the hosting providers.

Sharing, revocation and questions

Casa does not sell household data or include advertising analytics. Authorized household members can see the household information available through the app. You can revoke Casa’s Google authorization from your Google Account settings. Contact the household owner using the support address displayed on Google’s consent screen for access, correction, deletion or privacy questions.
